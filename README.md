Greetings, and welcome to a new project on Einsteins General Theory of Relativity.

This world is granted through a Physics-Based Simulated Realm in which you study, analyze, and manipulate the functions of our solar cosmos.

To Play, simply download the Windows Build or play on your Browser through: https://art1factual.itch.io/Gravitas
Then pan, tilt, and zoom through the realm, whilst analyzing the accurate systems of information upon the panel.

**Controls**: 

**F** - Focus

**WASD** - Move through Space
**
Shift+MouseWheel** - FlightSpeed

**Shift** - Accelerate

**Left Click** - Focus On a Planet

**Right Click** - Pan across the cosmic viewport.
